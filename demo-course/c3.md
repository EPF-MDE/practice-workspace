This is the Demo course: every service, number and answer in it is invented. C3 is one idea: rank errors by the harm they do, not by how often they show.

## Read the errors {#read-the-errors minutes=5}

**Worked Example: Pantry.** Pantry, an invented shopping-list app, logged two errors last week. Error A showed 900 times, for 3 people: a phone retrying in a loop. Error B showed 40 times, for 40 people, and each of them lost their list. Pantry fixed B first: it harmed 40 people, A only slowed down 3.

## Rank the errors {#rank-the-errors minutes=10}

Rank your service's errors, most harmful first, as Pantry did.

::: oceens
Lendly's error tracking, over the last seven days:

| Error                        | Occurrences | Readers |
| ---------------------------- | ----------- | ------- |
| X: the catalogue times out   | 300         | 150     |
| Y: a cover image is missing  | 2,000       | 20      |
| Z: a loan is confirmed twice | 12          | 12      |

::: field {#ranking kind=line}
Your ranking, most harmful first
:::

::: field {#why kind=text}
Why that order?
:::

::: field {#later kind=choice options="X|Y|Z"}
Which error would you watch next week?
:::

::: answer
**Z, then X, then Y.** Z gives one reader two copies and leaves another reader with none: real harm, each time. X makes 150 readers wait and try again. Y shows a grey square instead of a cover: annoying, and harmless. [The Demo's ranking notes](https://example.com/demo/lendly-ranking) say the same in a table.
:::
:::

::: mathutrice
Skywatch's error tracking, over the last seven days:

| Error                                           | Occurrences | Subscribers |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| P: the gateway refuses a warning, never retried | 8           | 8           |
| Q: a warning arrives twice                      | 500         | 250         |
| R: the sign-up page is slow                     | 3,000       | 90          |

::: field {#ranking kind=line}
Your ranking, most harmful first
:::

::: field {#why kind=text}
Why that order?
:::

::: field {#later kind=choice options="P|Q|R"}
Which error would you watch next week?
:::

::: answer
**P, then Q, then R.** P leaves 8 subscribers without a storm warning: the one harm Skywatch exists to prevent. Q annoys 250 people. R slows sign-up and harms no one already subscribed. [The Demo's ranking notes](https://example.com/demo/skywatch-ranking) say the same in a table.
:::
:::

## Follow the fix {#follow-the-fix minutes=10 after=rank-the-errors}

**Worked Example: Pantry's fix.** Pantry saved each list before sending it. The next day, error B showed 0 times, and error A still 900: the ranking held.

::: waiting
Send your ranking first: this step starts from it.
:::

::: oceens
Lendly fixed Z first: a loan now checks the copy is still free before it is confirmed. Over the next seven days, Z shows 0 times.

::: field {#after kind=line}
What does the error tracking show after the fix?
:::

::: field {#why-first kind=text}
Why fix Z before X?
:::

::: answer
**Why Z before X:** each Z took a book from a reader who had borrowed it, and X only made readers wait. After the fix, Z is gone and X is unchanged: the next one to fix.
:::
:::

::: mathutrice
Skywatch fixed P first: a refused warning now goes back to the queue. Over the next seven days, P shows 0 times.

::: field {#after kind=line}
What does the error tracking show after the fix?
:::

::: field {#why-first kind=text}
Why fix P before Q?
:::

::: answer
**Why P before Q:** each P left a subscriber without a storm warning, and Q only sent one twice. After the fix, P is gone and Q is unchanged: the next one to fix.
:::
:::
